About — photographer / visual creator working with people, music, fashion and film.

Luka Detiček is a photographer and visual creator from Ljubljana, Slovenia.

His work moves between editorials, portraits, live music, brand stories and film stills. The visual direction is minimal, raw and human — built around atmosphere, direct framing and real energy.

Available for portraits, campaigns, concerts, artist visuals, behind-the-scenes photography and selected photo / video projects.
